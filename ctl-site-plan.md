# CTL Roofing — Site Rebuild Plan

Planning notes for the multi-page rebuild. Client: CTL Pro Construction LLC (ctlpro.com), Broussard/Lafayette LA.

---

## Pages worth adding

Beyond the confirmed list (contact, gallery, services, meet the team, financing):

### Storm damage & insurance claims
The highest-value page currently missing. Half their Facebook content is storm response, and in Louisiana the real question a homeowner has isn't "who roofs" but "how does this work with my insurance." Cover the first 48 hours, what an adjuster looks for, what CTL does during the claim, and what's covered vs. not. It will rank and convert after every named storm, and it gives the gold storm strip on the home page somewhere to land.

### Areas we serve (hub + 6–10 town pages)
Lafayette, Broussard, Youngsville, New Iberia, Scott, Carencro, Breaux Bridge, Abbeville. The single biggest local-search lever for a trades company, and it makes "Committed to Local" structural instead of decorative. Each page: a couple of local projects, town-specific copy, same CTA.

### Project case studies (6–8)
This is what the "services page that indicates quality of work" ask actually needs. A gallery is for browsing; a case study sells. Format: problem → scope → materials → what it took → result, with 4–6 photos each. Link from the relevant service page and from the gallery.

### Reviews
Right now "Google" is a nav item that sends traffic off the site. Pull reviews in, keep people on the page, and put the "leave a review" CTA at the end for existing customers.

### Careers / join the crew
"We hire here" is their stated core value, and roofing companies recruit constantly. A simple page with a form pays for itself.

### Blog or advice section
Their Facebook copy is already good short-form content — "Don't ignore that water spot," "what's under a metal roof," "when a repair beats a replacement." Ten of those repurposed gives ranking pages and internal funnel material.

### Commercial
As a services child page. Property managers looking for TPO or silicone coatings shouldn't have to wade through residential shingle content.

### Lower priority
Warranty explainer · showroom page · video (fold their existing video content in as a gallery tab).

## Conversion mechanics to spec now

- **One primary action sitewide:** the free roof and property assessment. Every page ends in the same CTA band with page-specific copy — gallery says "want this on your house," financing says "see what you qualify for."
- **Sticky mobile bar:** Call | Text | Book. Traffic will be mostly mobile and often urgent.
- **Financing page needs an estimator** — a slider from project cost to estimated monthly payment. Keeps people on the page and pre-qualifies them psychologically. Requires the actual lender terms to build honestly.
- **Embed Calendly inline** on contact rather than bouncing to calendly.com.
- **Call tracking numbers** per channel, so they can see whether the site, Google Business Profile, or Facebook is producing.

## What we need from Robert before build

- Eight headshots shot the same way — same wall, same crop, same shirt — with name, role, and one line each
- LA contractor license number
- Manufacturer certifications, and specifically whether they're FORTIFIED-certified (one of the photos shows a FORTIFIED sign, and that's an insurance-discount trust badge in Louisiana)
- Financing partner name and real terms / prequalification link
- Definitive service-area town list
- Six to eight projects with before/after photos and basic details for case studies

## The decision that changes the quote

**Do they get a CMS?** They'll want to add gallery photos and projects themselves, and if they can't, we'll be doing it forever. Astro or Next with a lightweight CMS is the right call for a company this size — but it changes scope, price, and timeline, so decide before presenting.
